# AI Tells Audit — AgentleConsulting.com

_Caution: The site is cleaner than most AI-written sites. Many common tells (em-dashes, "leverage", "holistic", "cutting-edge", "empower") are absent. The writing is mostly human-passing. But the following tells are still present._

---

## Language Tells

### AI TELL #1: "We don't automate for the sake of it"
- **WHERE:** index.html → FAQ → "Which workflows should we automate first?" answer
- **CURRENT:** "We don't automate for the sake of it. We automate what actually moves the needle."
- **FIX:** Cut the first sentence entirely. "We automate what actually moves the needle" is fine on its own. The disclaimer "we don't do X for the sake of X" is a classic AI qualification pattern — it exists to pre-empt criticism rather than communicate anything real.

---

### AI TELL #2: "You don't need a dev team — we handle the technical"
- **WHERE:** index.html → Who We Serve → last bullet point
- **CURRENT:** "No dev team required — we handle the technical"
- **FIX:** "No dev team required" is fine. The em-dash + "we handle the technical" is vague and sounds like filler. Replace with something specific: "No dev team required. We handle the build, testing, and handover."

---

### AI TELL #3: "Just 30 minutes" construction
- **WHERE:** index.html → CTA sections (multiple)
- **CURRENT:** "Just 30 minutes mapping your highest-ROI automation opportunities" / "Just 30 minutes figuring out which package fits"
- **FIX:** "Just" diminishes the call and reads as AI-generated minimising. Replace with: "30 minutes mapping your highest-ROI automation opportunities." Don't qualify it.

- **WHERE:** index.html → CTA text (repeated 3+ times)

### AI TELL #20: All trust stats are suspiciously round
- **WHERE:** index.html → Trust Strip + about page stats
- **CURRENT:** "100% Ownership", "40+ Businesses helped", "100% Systems still running", "1.1mo Avg. ROI payback", "10–20h Recovered per week"

### AI TELL #23: Pricing table has very round "approximate" costs
