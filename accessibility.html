Accessibility

Our commitment to making AgentleConsulting.com usable for everyone.

Our Commitment

AgentleConsulting is committed to ensuring that our website is accessible to all users, including people with disabilities. We aim to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at the AA level.

What We're Doing

  • All images include descriptive alt text
  • Color contrast meets WCAG AA minimum ratios
  • The site is navigable by keyboard alone
  • Font sizes are resizable up to 200% without loss of content
  • Forms have associated labels and error messaging
  • Page language is set and consistent

Known Limitations

Some older third-party integrations (e.g., Calendly for booking) may not fully inherit our accessibility standards. We are working with our vendors to improve this. If you experience difficulty using any part of our site, please contact us.

Feedback

If you encounter any accessibility barrier on our site, contact us at hello@agentleconsulting.com. We take accessibility feedback seriously and aim to respond within 3 business days.
