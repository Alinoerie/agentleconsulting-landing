Case Study

Payback in 4.5 Months

How a Dutch Amazon+Bol.com seller automated their cross-platform inventory sync and supplier workflow — and stopped chasing stock manually.

Client
BOVO (NL)
Platforms
Amazon EU + Bol.com
Workflows Automated
2 core processes
Payback Period
4.5 months

The Problem

BOVO manages inventory across Amazon EU and Bol.com — two platforms with different listing formats, pricing rules, and stock pools. Every time a supplier delivered new stock or a sale happened on one platform, their team had to manually update the other.

Worse: supplier requests for quotes (RFQs) arrived by email and had to be retyped into a spreadsheet, checked against current margins, and replied to manually. The team was spending hours every day on data entry — not on growing the business.

The Solution

We built two n8n workflows that ran on triggers, not schedules:

  • Cross-platform inventory sync — when stock updates on Amazon, Bol.com listing adjusts automatically. No manual re-entry. No oversells.
  • Supplier RFQ pipeline — inbound emails are parsed, matched against margin rules, and formatted into a response draft. One click to send or adjust.

Both workflows were built in n8n — BOVO's team now owns and can edit them without depending on us. That's the Handover-First Framework in practice.
