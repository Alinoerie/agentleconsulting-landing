# FAQ Additions — AgentleConsulting
## Ali's voice: direct, honest, no sales spin

---

### Which specific workflows should we automate first?

We start with whatever costs you the most time and creates the most errors. For most clients, that's order processing and customer notifications — because everyone has it, everyone does it manually, and it generates a constant low-level drain. From there, we look at your specific bottlenecks: inventory reconciliation, supplier follow-ups, reporting. We don't automate things that don't have a clear time or error cost.

The way we decide: you show us where you spend your time, we estimate the ROI of automating each thing, and you choose. We don't build things you haven't approved.

---

### How long does a typical automation project take from kickoff to go-live?

For a scoped automation project (3–5 workflows): 4–8 weeks from the day you approve the quote. Simpler standalone automations: 1–2 weeks.

The variance is in the complexity of your existing setup. If you have clean data sources and clear processes, we move fast. If you're working with a legacy ERP, three disconnected platforms, and undocumented processes, it takes longer — and we'll tell you that upfront before we start.

We don't disappear for six weeks and come back with something unexpected. You'll see weekly progress updates.

---

### Who owns the automation once the project is done?

You do. Everything we build is documented and handed over at the end of the engagement. Workflow files, API credentials, runbooks — all of it. If you want to host it yourself, export it, or hand it to another developer, you can.

We use standard, open tools where possible: n8n, Make, standard APIs. We don't build on proprietary platforms designed to trap clients. If we ever stopped existing tomorrow, your automation would still run.

---

### What happens if the automation doesn't deliver the promised savings?

We test everything before we call it done. If a workflow doesn't perform as specified in the project scope, we fix it — that's included in the project fee. We don't hand over something broken and invoice.

What we can't guarantee is that your team will actually use it. Automation only delivers ROI if people adopt it. We do adoption support as part of every engagement, but if your team decides to work around the automation, the savings won't materialize. That's why we spend time on training and change management — not just building.

---

### How do you handle data privacy and GDPR compliance?

Everything we build respects GDPR and any other applicable data protection regulation. We don't move data unnecessarily, we don't store personal data in places it doesn't need to be, and we document where data lives in your system.

If you have an existing GDPR obligation (which most EU businesses do), the automation we build for you needs to fit within that. We ask about your data handling setup at the start of every project. If your current data architecture has GDPR gaps, we'll flag them — but cleaning those up is typically a separate piece of work.

### Do you offer a pilot or proof-of-concept before a full engagement?

Yes — sort of. We don't do free pilots because they require significant uncompensated work. What we do offer is the Discover package: a focused 2-week sprint that maps your automation opportunities and delivers 2 working demo workflows. You see exactly what we're building and how it performs before you commit to anything larger.

If the demo doesn't convince you, you walk away with a prioritized roadmap and a €500 credit toward Automate. No obligation.

### What makes you different from other AI consultants we've spoken to?

Three things most clients mention:

First, we build before we charge. Most consultants send a proposal, take a retainer, and disappear for three months. We scope, quote, get approval, and build — in that order. You see progress every week.

Second, we hand over everything. We've seen clients who paid €30,000 for automation retainers and at the end couldn't export their own workflows. We don't do that.

Third, we don't oversell AI. If a spreadsheet formula solves your problem faster than an AI agent, we'll tell you. We're not paid to sell AI — we're paid to solve operational problems. Sometimes AI is the right tool. Sometimes it isn't.

### How do you get our employees to actually use the automation (not work around it)?

The honest answer: adoption is your job as much as ours. We can build the best automation in the world, but if your team is resistant, it won't get used.

What we do: we build with your team's actual workflow in mind — not an idealized version of it. We test with real users before go-live. We document everything in plain language, not technical jargon. And we do a structured handover session where the people who'll use it day-to-day get hands-on time with the system.

What we can't do: force people to use it. If you have a team that's set in their ways and a manager who isn't behind the change, automation projects fail. We flag that risk before we take on an engagement — and we'll be honest with you about whether we're the right fit for your situation.
